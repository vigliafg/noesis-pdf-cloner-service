## 2. Data processed

- **Uploaded documents** (PDF files) and the **text** they contain, needed for

## 4. Recipients and transfers

To translate, the **text of the documents** is sent to the chosen engines:

- **Google** (free chain),
- **Microsoft / Bing**,
- **OpenRouter** (LLM engine, with the user-provided key).

These providers may be located **outside the European Union** (e.g., USA).
Transfers rely on the tools provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses /
adequacy decisions) and on those providers' own terms. The Service provider does
not control those services.

We do not sell personal data. Data may be disclosed to authorities when required
by law.

## 5. Retention

- **Documents**: automatically removed after **[DOCUMENT_RETENTION_HOURS]**
  (default 24 hours) from last use.
- **Job artifacts and logs**: removed after **[JOB_RETENTION_HOURS]** (default
  72 hours).
- **Terms acceptance and security logs**: kept as long as needed to demonstrate
  compliance, approximately up to **12 months**.
- **Translation cache**: holds translation results; it can be cleared by the
  controller and is not linked to a user profile.
